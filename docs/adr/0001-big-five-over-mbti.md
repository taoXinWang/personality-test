# ADR 0001: Use Big Five Personality Model Over MBTI

## Status
Accepted

## Context
We need to choose a psychological framework for measuring personality traits. The two most popular models are:

1. **MBTI (Myers-Briggs Type Indicator)**: 4 dimensions creating 16 discrete types
2. **Big Five (OCEAN)**: 5 dimensions measured on continuous scales

16personalities.com uses MBTI as its foundation (with a 5th dimension added), which has proven successful for viral growth. However, MBTI has significant scientific criticism:
- Low test-retest reliability
- Forced binary classification (you're either E or I, no middle ground)
- Limited predictive validity in academic research

The Big Five model is the most scientifically validated personality framework, with decades of cross-cultural research supporting its stability and predictive power.

## Decision
We will use the **Big Five personality model** as our foundation, but present it in a user-friendly way similar to 16personalities:

- **5 dimensions**: Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, Neuroticism
- **Renamed for accessibility**: Exploration, Structure, Social Energy, Harmony, Resilience
- **16 archetypes**: Generated by 4 dimensions (not 5), to maintain the "16 types" mental model that users expect
- **Continuous scoring**: Each dimension scored 0-100%, not binary
- **Type assignment**: Use 50% threshold to assign archetype, but show preference strength

## Consequences

### Positive
- **Scientific credibility**: Big Five is academically respected, which matters for B2B expansion and media coverage
- **True differentiation**: 16personalities is "MBTI with extra steps" — we offer something genuinely different
- **Measurement precision**: Continuous scales capture nuance that binary types miss
- **Cross-cultural validity**: Big Five has been validated across dozens of languages and cultures

### Negative
- **Less familiar branding**: MBTI has 80 years of cultural penetration; Big Five is less known to general audiences
- **Naming challenge**: Terms like "Neuroticism" and "Agreeableness" need careful translation to avoid negative connotations
- **Educational burden**: Users may need more explanation of what each dimension means

### Mitigations
- Use accessible dimension names (Exploration, not Openness)
- Maintain the "16 archetypes" structure so users still get a type label
- Provide clear, jargon-free descriptions of each dimension
- Emphasize the continuous nature as a feature ("more accurate than binary tests")

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Pure MBTI
Clone 16personalities' approach exactly.

**Rejected because**: No differentiation, and MBTI's scientific weaknesses could hurt credibility long-term.

### Alternative 2: Enneagram
Use the 9-type Enneagram system.

**Rejected because**: Less research support than Big Five, and the spiritual/mystical associations may not fit our brand.

### Alternative 3: HEXACO
Use the 6-dimension HEXACO model (Big Five + Honesty-Humility).

**Rejected because**: 6 dimensions creates 64 possible types (2^6), which is too many for users to grasp. The "16 types" mental model is a sweet spot.
