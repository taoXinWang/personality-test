# 16 Personality Archetypes - Definitions

This document defines the 16 personality archetypes generated by the four type-generating dimensions.

## Dimension Encoding

Each archetype has a 4-letter code based on the high/low poles of each dimension:

| Dimension | High Pole (≥50%) | Low Pole (<50%) |
|-----------|------------------|-----------------|
| **Exploration** | I (Innovator 创新者) | P (Pragmatist 务实者) |
| **Structure** | L (pLanner 计划者) | S (Spontaneous 自由人) |
| **Social Energy** | O (Outgoing 外向者) | R (Reserved 内敛者) |
| **Harmony** | H (Harmonizer 协调者) | C (Challenger 挑战者) |

**Note**: We use "L" for pLanner (not "P") to avoid confusion with Pragmatist.

## The 16 Archetypes

### 1. ILOH - The Visionary Harmonizer (远见协调者)
**Profile**: Innovator + Planner + Outgoing + Harmonizer

Creative and organized, socially engaged, seeks harmony. Brings people together around new ideas with structured execution.

**Workplace**: Natural project leader who balances innovation with planning. Excels at change management and team alignment.

**Romance**: Seeks deep connection with shared vision. Plans thoughtful experiences, values emotional harmony.

**Social**: The friend who organizes group activities around new experiences. Mediates conflicts, builds community.

## Type Modifiers

Each archetype can have a type modifier based on the Resilience dimension:

- **-S (Stable 稳定型)**: Resilience ≥50%
  - Emotionally steady, confident, handles stress well
  - Less affected by criticism or setbacks
  - Maintains consistent mood and energy

- **-T (Turbulent 敏感型)**: Resilience <50%
  - More emotionally sensitive, self-aware, driven by improvement
  - More affected by stress and criticism
  - Higher emotional range, more introspective

**Example**: 
- **ILOH-S**: Visionary Harmonizer who stays calm under pressure
- **ILOH-T**: Visionary Harmonizer who is more emotionally attuned and self-critical

The modifier doesn't change the core archetype, but affects how it's expressed.
